# Code of conduct

Besh welcomes people of every background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and clear.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Accept useful feedback and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Help others participate. Respect privacy and boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual attention, doxxing, impersonation, and repeated disruption are not allowed. Do not post another person's private information.

These rules apply in project spaces and when representing Besh elsewhere.

## Reporting and response

Contact a repository maintainer through a private contact method published on their GitHub profile. Do not report private incidents in public issues. A dedicated reporting address must be configured before the project opens a public community; none is claimed here.

Maintainers will handle reports as privately as practical, check evidence, hear affected people where appropriate, and avoid retaliation. Responses may include a warning, content removal, a temporary restriction, or a permanent ban. Serious threats or privacy violations can result in immediate restrictions.

Appeals may be sent privately to a different maintainer when available. Maintainers involved in a complaint should not decide its outcome.
